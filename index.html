Chapter 03  ·  Project 01

Sweepstake
Pool

A decentralised Ethereum lottery where users deposit ETH for a proportional chance to win the pool. Chainlink Automation monitors the contract and triggers a draw once the interval elapses. Chainlink VRF then selects a verifiably random winner — no admin control, no manipulation. Rewards transfer automatically and the pool resets for the next round.

Chapter 03  ·  Project 03

P2P Marketplace

A trustless peer-to-peer marketplace powered by on-chain escrow. Buyers lock funds into a smart contract on purchase; the seller only receives payment once the buyer confirms delivery — no middlemen, no custodial risk. Dispute logic and fund release are handled entirely by the contract, keeping every transaction transparent and tamper-proof. This currently runs on ETH Sepolia testnet using USDC - 0x1c7D4B196Cb0C7B01d743Fbc6116a902379C7238 (token address)
